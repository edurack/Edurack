# NEET Physics Atoms PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Atoms (NEET Physics): 12 questions in 2020–2026, 3.1% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Atoms gave **12 of the 387** NEET Physics questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.1% of the subject and the **#15 chapter out of 30** by weightage, about 1.5 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2023.

Of the 12 questions tagged for difficulty, 33% were easy, 59% medium and 8% hard, so this is mostly medium-level questions that reward practice beyond one-line facts.

It has been asked more often lately: 1.7 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 0.7 per paper in 2020–2022.
