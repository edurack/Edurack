# NEET Chemistry Electrochemistry PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Electrochemistry (NEET Chemistry): 15 questions in 2020–2026, 3.8% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Electrochemistry gave **15 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.8% of the subject and the **#12 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 1.9 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2022.

Of the 15 questions tagged for difficulty, 13% were easy, 40% medium and 47% hard, so this is a chapter that tends to bring the tougher questions.

Its weight has stayed steady: 2.3 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 1.7 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
