Oscillations gave **14 of the 387** NEET Physics questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.6% of the subject and the **#12 chapter out of 30** by weightage, about 1.8 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2026.

It has been asked more often lately: 2.3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 1.3 per paper in 2020–2022.
