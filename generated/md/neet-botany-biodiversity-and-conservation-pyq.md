# NEET Botany Biodiversity and Conservation PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Biodiversity and Conservation (NEET Botany): 20 questions in 2020–2026, 4.9% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Biodiversity and Conservation gave **20 of the 405** NEET Botany questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 4.9% of the subject and the **#11 chapter out of 21** by weightage, about 2.5 questions per paper. It peaked at 5 in NEET 2024.

Of the 20 questions tagged for difficulty, 65% were easy, 20% medium and 15% hard, so this is a chapter where NCERT-level preparation pays off directly.

It has been asked more often lately: 2.7 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 1.7 per paper in 2020–2022.
