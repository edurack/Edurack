# NEET Physics Gravitation PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Gravitation (NEET Physics): 14 questions in 2020–2026, 3.6% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Gravitation gave **14 of the 387** NEET Physics questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.6% of the subject and the **#11 chapter out of 30** by weightage, about 1.8 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2021.

Of the 14 questions tagged for difficulty, 29% were easy, 50% medium and 21% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

Its weight has stayed steady: 1.7 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 1.7 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
