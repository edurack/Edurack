Organisms and Populations gave **22 of the 405** NEET Botany questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 5.4% of the subject and the **#9 chapter out of 21** by weightage, about 2.8 questions per paper. It peaked at 4 in NEET 2021.

It has been asked more often lately: 3.3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 2.3 per paper in 2020–2022.
