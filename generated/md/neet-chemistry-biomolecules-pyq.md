# NEET Chemistry Biomolecules PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Biomolecules (NEET Chemistry): 14 questions in 2020–2026, 3.5% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Biomolecules gave **14 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.5% of the subject and the **#13 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 1.8 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2020.

Of the 14 questions tagged for difficulty, 29% were easy, 64% medium and 7% hard, so this is mostly medium-level questions that reward practice beyond one-line facts.

Its weight has stayed steady: 1.7 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 2 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
