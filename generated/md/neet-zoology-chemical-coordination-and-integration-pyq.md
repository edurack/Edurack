# NEET Zoology Chemical Coordination and Integration PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Chemical Coordination and Integration (NEET Zoology): 15 questions in 2020–2026, 3.9% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Chemical Coordination and Integration gave **15 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.9% of the subject and the **#12 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 1.9 questions per paper. It peaked at 4 in NEET 2025.

Of the 15 questions tagged for difficulty, 53% were easy, 34% medium and 13% hard, so this is a chapter where NCERT-level preparation pays off directly.

It has been asked more often lately: 2.7 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 1.3 per paper in 2020–2022.
