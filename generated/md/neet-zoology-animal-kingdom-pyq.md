# NEET Zoology Animal Kingdom PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Animal Kingdom (NEET Zoology): 38 questions in 2020–2026, 9.8% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Animal Kingdom gave **38 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 9.8% of the subject and the **#2 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 4.8 questions per paper. It peaked at 12 in NEET 2021.

Of the 38 questions tagged for difficulty, 42% were easy, 47% medium and 11% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

It has been asked less often lately: 4 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 6.3 per paper in 2020–2022.
