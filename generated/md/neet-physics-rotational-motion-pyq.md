# NEET Physics Rotational Motion PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Rotational Motion (NEET Physics): 15 questions in 2020–2026, 3.9% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Rotational Motion gave **15 of the 387** NEET Physics questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.9% of the subject and the **#9 chapter out of 30** by weightage, about 1.9 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2025.

Of the 15 questions tagged for difficulty, 27% were easy, 46% medium and 27% hard, so this is a chapter that tends to bring the tougher questions.

It has been asked more often lately: 2.3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 1.7 per paper in 2020–2022.
