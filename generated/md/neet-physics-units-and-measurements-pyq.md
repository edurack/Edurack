> Units and Measurements (NEET Physics): 22 questions in 2020–2026, 5.7% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Units and Measurements gave **22 of the 387** NEET Physics questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 5.7% of the subject and the **#4 chapter out of 30** by weightage, about 2.8 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2020.

Its weight has stayed steady: 3 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 2.7 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
