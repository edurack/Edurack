# NEET Physics Current Electricity PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Current Electricity (NEET Physics): 34 questions in 2020–2026, 8.8% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Current Electricity gave **34 of the 387** NEET Physics questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 8.8% of the subject and the **#1 chapter out of 30** by weightage, about 4.3 questions per paper. It peaked at 6 in NEET 2020.

Of the 34 questions tagged for difficulty, 29% were easy, 62% medium and 9% hard, so this is mostly medium-level questions that reward practice beyond one-line facts.

It has been asked less often lately: 3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 5.7 per paper in 2020–2022.
