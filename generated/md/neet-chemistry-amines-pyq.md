# NEET Chemistry Amines PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Amines (NEET Chemistry): 18 questions in 2020–2026, 4.5% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Amines gave **18 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 4.5% of the subject and the **#9 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 2.3 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2021.

Of the 18 questions tagged for difficulty, 11% were easy, 72% medium and 17% hard, so this is mostly medium-level questions that reward practice beyond one-line facts.

It has been asked more often lately: 2.7 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 2 per paper in 2020–2022.
