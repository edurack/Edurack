> Hydrocarbons (NEET Chemistry): 21 questions in 2020–2026, 5.3% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Hydrocarbons gave **21 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 5.3% of the subject and the **#4 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 2.6 questions per paper. It peaked at 4 in NEET 2021.

Of the 21 questions tagged for difficulty, 24% were easy, 52% medium and 24% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

Its weight has stayed steady: 3 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 2.7 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
