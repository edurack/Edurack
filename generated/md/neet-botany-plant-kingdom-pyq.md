> Plant Kingdom (NEET Botany): 23 questions in 2020–2026, 5.7% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Plant Kingdom gave **23 of the 405** NEET Botany questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 5.7% of the subject and the **#8 chapter out of 21** by weightage, about 2.9 questions per paper. It peaked at 5 in NEET 2021.

Of the 22 questions tagged for difficulty, 55% were easy, 18% medium and 27% hard, so this is a chapter where NCERT-level preparation pays off directly.

Its weight has stayed steady: 3.7 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 2.7 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
