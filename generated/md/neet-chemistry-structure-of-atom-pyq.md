# NEET Chemistry Structure of Atom PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Structure of Atom (NEET Chemistry): 13 questions in 2020–2026, 3.3% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Structure of Atom gave **13 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.3% of the subject and the **#15 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 1.6 questions per paper. It peaked at 2 in NEET 2021.

Of the 13 questions tagged for difficulty, 31% were easy, 46% medium and 23% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

Its weight has stayed steady: 1.7 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 1.7 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
