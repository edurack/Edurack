# NEET Zoology Biomolecules PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Biomolecules (NEET Zoology): 38 questions in 2020–2026, 9.8% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Biomolecules gave **38 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 9.8% of the subject and the **#3 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 4.8 questions per paper. It peaked at 6 in NEET 2020.

Of the 38 questions tagged for difficulty, 37% were easy, 45% medium and 18% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

It has been asked less often lately: 3.7 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 5.7 per paper in 2020–2022.
