# NEET Zoology Reproductive Health PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Reproductive Health (NEET Zoology): 23 questions in 2020–2026, 5.9% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Reproductive Health gave **23 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 5.9% of the subject and the **#10 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 2.9 questions per paper. It peaked at 5 in NEET 2022.

Of the 23 questions tagged for difficulty, 39% were easy, 57% medium and 4% hard, so this is mostly medium-level questions that reward practice beyond one-line facts.

It has been asked less often lately: 1.7 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 3.7 per paper in 2020–2022.
