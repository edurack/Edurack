# NEET Chemistry Thermodynamics PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Thermodynamics (NEET Chemistry): 16 questions in 2020–2026, 4% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Thermodynamics gave **16 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 4% of the subject and the **#11 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 2 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2024.

Of the 16 questions tagged for difficulty, 19% were easy, 43% medium and 38% hard, so this is a chapter that tends to bring the tougher questions.

Its weight has stayed steady: 1.7 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 2 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
