# NEET Zoology Evolution PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Evolution (NEET Zoology): 25 questions in 2020–2026, 6.4% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Evolution gave **25 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 6.4% of the subject and the **#7 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 3.1 questions per paper. It peaked at 6 in Re-NEET 2026.

Of the 25 questions tagged for difficulty, 48% were easy, 44% medium and 8% hard, so this is a chapter where NCERT-level preparation pays off directly.

Its weight has stayed steady: 3.3 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 3.3 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
