# NEET Botany Ecosystem PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Ecosystem (NEET Botany): 19 questions in 2020–2026, 4.7% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Ecosystem gave **19 of the 405** NEET Botany questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 4.7% of the subject and the **#13 chapter out of 21** by weightage, about 2.4 questions per paper. It peaked at 4 in NEET 2025.

Of the 19 questions tagged for difficulty, 26% were easy, 53% medium and 21% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

Its weight has stayed steady: 2.3 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 3 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
