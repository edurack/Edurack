> Molecular Basis of Inheritance (NEET Botany): 47 questions in 2020–2026, 11.6% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Molecular Basis of Inheritance gave **47 of the 405** NEET Botany questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 11.6% of the subject and the **#1 chapter out of 21** by weightage, about 5.9 questions per paper. It peaked at 10 in NEET 2021.

Of the 47 questions tagged for difficulty, 47% were easy, 40% medium and 13% hard, so this is a chapter where NCERT-level preparation pays off directly.

It has been asked less often lately: 4.3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 7 per paper in 2020–2022.
