# NEET Botany Principles of Inheritance and Variation PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Principles of Inheritance and Variation (NEET Botany): 36 questions in 2020–2026, 8.9% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Principles of Inheritance and Variation gave **36 of the 405** NEET Botany questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 8.9% of the subject and the **#2 chapter out of 21** by weightage, about 4.5 questions per paper. It peaked at 7 in NEET 2026.

Of the 36 questions tagged for difficulty, 33% were easy, 50% medium and 17% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

Its weight has stayed steady: 4 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 4.3 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
