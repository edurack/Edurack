Morphology of Flowering Plants gave **27 of the 405** NEET Botany questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 6.7% of the subject and the **#5 chapter out of 21** by weightage, about 3.4 questions per paper. It peaked at 5 in NEET 2024.

Its weight has stayed steady: 3 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 3.3 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
