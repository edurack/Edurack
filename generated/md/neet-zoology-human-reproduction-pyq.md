# NEET Zoology Human Reproduction PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Human Reproduction (NEET Zoology): 32 questions in 2020–2026, 8.2% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Human Reproduction gave **32 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 8.2% of the subject and the **#4 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 4 questions per paper. It peaked at 5 in NEET 2023.

Of the 32 questions tagged for difficulty, 25% were easy, 53% medium and 22% hard, so this is a fairly balanced mix of easy and moderate questions.

It has been asked more often lately: 4.3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 3 per paper in 2020–2022.
