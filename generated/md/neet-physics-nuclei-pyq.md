# NEET Physics Nuclei PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Nuclei (NEET Physics): 13 questions in 2020–2026, 3.4% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Nuclei gave **13 of the 387** NEET Physics questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 3.4% of the subject and the **#14 chapter out of 30** by weightage, about 1.6 questions per paper. It peaked at 3 in NEET 2020.

Of the 13 questions tagged for difficulty, 69% were easy, 31% medium and 0% hard, so this is a chapter where NCERT-level preparation pays off directly.

It has been asked less often lately: 1 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 2.7 per paper in 2020–2022.
