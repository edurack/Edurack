# NEET Zoology Biotechnology and its Applications PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Biotechnology and its Applications (NEET Zoology): 27 questions in 2020–2026, 7% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Biotechnology and its Applications gave **27 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 7% of the subject and the **#6 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 3.4 questions per paper. It peaked at 7 in NEET 2021.

Of the 27 questions tagged for difficulty, 33% were easy, 63% medium and 4% hard, so this is mostly medium-level questions that reward practice beyond one-line facts.

It has been asked less often lately: 3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 4.3 per paper in 2020–2022.
