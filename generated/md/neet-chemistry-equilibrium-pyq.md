# NEET Chemistry Equilibrium PYQ Analysis 2020–2026: Topic-wise Trends & Practice

> Equilibrium (NEET Chemistry): 19 questions in 2020–2026, 4.8% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Equilibrium gave **19 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 4.8% of the subject and the **#8 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 2.4 questions per paper. It peaked at 4 in NEET 2026.

Of the 19 questions tagged for difficulty, 21% were easy, 47% medium and 32% hard, so this is a chapter that tends to bring the tougher questions.

It has been asked more often lately: 2.7 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 2 per paper in 2020–2022.
