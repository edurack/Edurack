> The Solid State (NEET Chemistry): 7 questions in 2020–2026, 1.8% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

The Solid State gave **7 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 1.8% of the subject and the **#25 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 0.9 questions per paper. It peaked at 2 in NEET 2021.
