> Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids (NEET Chemistry): 26 questions in 2020–2026, 6.5% of the subject. Topic-wise trends, difficulty split, repeated topics and PYQ practice.

Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids gave **26 of the 398** NEET Chemistry questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 6.5% of the subject and the **#1 chapter out of 31** by weightage, about 3.3 questions per paper. It peaked at 5 in NEET 2022.

Of the 26 questions tagged for difficulty, 8% were easy, 50% medium and 42% hard, so this is a chapter that tends to bring the tougher questions.

It has been asked less often lately: 2.3 questions per paper across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026, against 4 per paper in 2020–2022.
