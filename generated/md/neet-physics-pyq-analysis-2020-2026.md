**How many NEET Physics questions were asked from 2020–2026?**

387 Physics questions across 8 papers (2020 to 2026, plus Re-NEET 2026), spread over 30 chapters.

**Which NEET Physics chapter has the highest weightage?**

Current Electricity has the highest weightage with 34 questions (8.8%). Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance follows with 26.

**Which Physics chapters are easiest to score in?**

**How many Physics chapters cover most of the paper?**

The top 10 chapters give about half of all questions and the top 19 give about 80%.
