Body Fluids and Circulation gave **18 of the 388** NEET Zoology questions asked between 2020 and 2026 (Re-NEET 2026 included). That is 4.6% of the subject and the **#11 chapter out of 16** by weightage, about 2.3 questions per paper. It peaked at 4 in NEET 2022.

Its weight has stayed steady: 2.7 questions per paper in 2020–2022 and 2 across 2025, 2026 and Re-NEET 2026.
